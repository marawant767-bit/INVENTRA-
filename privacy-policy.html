Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026
This Privacy Policy describes how the INVENTRA mobile application handles information. INVENTRA is designed to work locally on your device. Some optional features may send information to a service that you choose and configure yourself.

1. Information stored on your device

INVENTRA stores app information such as project details, learning progress, preferences, and user-created content in local device storage (including browser-style local storage). This information is used to provide the app's features and is not automatically sent to the app developer by the local-only features.

2. Optional cloud synchronization

The app includes an optional cloud-save feature that can connect to a Supabase project. It does not connect to a developer-operated Supabase project by default. If you choose to enter your own Supabase project URL and public/anonymous key and enable synchronization, the data needed for that feature may be sent to and stored by that Supabase project. The Supabase project operator's privacy terms and settings will apply to that data.

3. Optional AI connection

The app can be configured with an AI backend endpoint. The AI assistant remains local unless you provide and use an endpoint. If you choose to connect one and send a message, the message (and any conversation content included in the request) is transmitted to that endpoint for processing. The operator of that endpoint may process or retain the information under its own policies. Do not submit passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

4. Information sharing

INVENTRA does not include advertising in the supplied app configuration and does not sell personal information. Information is only transmitted when you use an optional network feature, such as cloud synchronization or an AI endpoint that you configure, or when required by law.

5. Permissions and network access

The supplied Android app configuration requests internet access, network-state access, and vibration. Internet access is used for optional network features and connectivity. The app configuration supplied for review does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions.

6. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains on your device until you delete it within the app (where supported), clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling may permanently remove local data. If you enabled cloud synchronization, local deletion may not delete copies already stored in your own Supabase project; manage or delete those copies through that project's dashboard. Data sent to an AI endpoint is subject to that endpoint operator's retention practices.

7. Children's privacy

INVENTRA is a general-purpose creative and productivity tool. It is not designed to collect personal information from children. Users should avoid entering personal or sensitive information into project content, cloud services, or third-party AI services.

8. Security

We aim to keep the app's local and optional data flows limited to the features described above. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device and for reviewing the security and privacy settings of any third-party service you connect.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact method provided on the app's official store listing. A dedicated privacy contact email has not been published in this policy.
